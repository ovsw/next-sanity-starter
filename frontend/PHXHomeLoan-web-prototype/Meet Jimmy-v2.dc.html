THE EARLY YEARS

The son of a salesman, forged as a Marine

Jimmy grew up in the San Francisco Bay Area, the son of a produce salesman and a waitress. His parents divorced when he was five, and he spent much of childhood moving between two homes. By his own telling, nothing marked him as exceptional.

One conviction did take hold early: how you make people feel matters more than how much you know. At 18, Jimmy enlisted in the Marine Corps infantry. His service took him around the world and ultimately to Iraq in support of Operation Iraqi Freedom.

“Deep down, I always believed that how you made people feel matters more than how much you know.”

A NEW MISSION

A girl from Arizona, and a new way to serve

One night in Hawaii, Jimmy met Sylvia while both were navigating recent losses in their families. The next morning, he called his sister and said, “I just met my future wife.” He could not explain it. He simply knew.

Jimmy finished his service, moved to Arizona, and married her. Twenty years and two children later, they have built a life here. Around that same time, a conversation with his sister-in-law introduced Jimmy to mortgages—and to a new way of serving people.

“Once a Marine, always a Marine!”
Jimmy's favorite phrase — and the standard he brings to every family he serves
DISCIPLINE, APPLIED

Lessons from the Corps, applied to lending

Jimmy entered the mortgage business without a degree, a finance background, or industry connections. He walked door-to-door along Camelback Road until one company finally told him, “We’ll give you a shot, Marine.”

The early years were lean, but Jimmy discovered that his greatest advantage was teaching. He founded a real estate school, began answering mortgage questions on YouTube, and built a practice around making complicated decisions easier to understand.

Mortgage education

Through his real estate school and free video library, Jimmy has helped thousands of real estate agents better understand the lending process.

A trusted industry voice

Jimmy’s teaching has also taken him to the stage, including an invitation to speak at the Todd Duncan Sales Mastery Event.

Thousands
OF REAL ESTATE AGENTS EDUCATED

The real estate school and YouTube channel Jimmy started have educated thousands of agents — many of whom now send him their buyers. Two decades later, that compound effect has grown into a mortgage practice spanning the nation, built on teaching, speaking, word of mouth, and satisfied customers.

ON YOUTUBE

1,000,000 views — one question at a time

Jimmy never set out to be a YouTuber. He set out to answer the questions veterans actually ask — about VA loans, credit, refinancing, and buying a first home. Hundreds of videos later, the channel just crossed one million views.

Every video is free, and none of it is a sales pitch. It's the same advice he gives across the desk — recorded once, so it can serve a million people instead of one.
