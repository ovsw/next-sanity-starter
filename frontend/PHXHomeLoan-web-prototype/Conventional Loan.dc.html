THE BASICS

What are conventional mortgage loans?

Conventional mortgage loans are part of a loan program unique to private lenders — banks, credit unions, and mortgage companies. Because no government agency controls them, no agency can charge fees on them either.

Most conventional mortgage loans have fixed rates that do not change during the life of the loan, although some are adjustable-rate mortgages. Many buyers are drawn to these loans because interest rates stay stable in the midst of unpredictable times.

The seller can help with closing costs

On a conventional loan, the seller can cover closing costs up to 3% of the house's value — money that stays in your pocket at the closing table.

TWO CATEGORIES

What kinds of conventional mortgage loans are there?

While conventional mortgage loans are the same by nature, a few key differences set them apart. Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac set dollar limits on conventional loans — where your loan falls relative to those limits determines its category.

THREE KEY ADVANTAGES

What are the advantages of conventional mortgage loans?

Among the countless financing options available, conventional mortgage loans offer key advantages that make them a wise home loan decision.

01

They allow for a second home purchase

Other programs such as VA Home Loans only allow the purchase of a primary residence. Conventional mortgage loans give you greater versatility — invest in a home to sell in the future, or buy a vacation home for your family.

02

They give the choice of mortgage insurance

While FHA loans require lifetime mortgage insurance, conventional home loan participants only need it temporarily. Once the home's principal has been paid below 78%, you can decide to bypass the monthly payments entirely.

03

They stop additional program fees

Because private lenders lead conventional mortgage loans, home buyers are protected from hidden government fees. Government-sponsored programs apply funding fees instead — VA Home Loans, for example, demand a 1.4 to 2.3% funding fee.

ELIGIBILITY

What are conventional mortgage loan requirements?

Conventional mortgage loans carry incredible benefits for homebuyers but come with stricter requirements. To be eligible, you have to pay a certain down payment, keep a high enough credit score, and have a promising debt-to-income ratio.

REQUIREMENT 01

Down payment

First-time buyers may qualify with only a 3% down payment. In all other situations, the figure depends on your home buying status and the kind of conventional loan:

First-time home buyers 3%
Experienced buyers, or income under 80% of your area's median 5%
Adjustable-rate mortgages 5%
Buying a second home 10%
Single-family homes 15%
Jumbo loans 20–40%
REQUIREMENT 02

Credit score & DTI

Since interest rates run lower for conventional mortgage loans than VA loans, your credit score has to be higher to compensate. And unlike most misconceptions, you can still have debt and qualify.
